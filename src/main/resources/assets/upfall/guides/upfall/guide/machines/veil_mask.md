# Veil Mask

<ItemImage id="veil_mask" scale="4" float="right" />

The **Veil Mask** is a helmet that filters the Veil Gas found in gas caves. While you wear it the gas cannot hurt you, and the gas becomes visible.

## Crafting

Smith an Iron Helmet, a <ItemLink id="kairite_gear" /> and a [Veil Mask Blueprint](index.md).

<RecipeFor id="veil_mask" />

## Using it

Veil Gas hurts you only when it touches your head, so the mask is what lets you explore gas caves.

The mask is also one of the offerings for the [Veilsight](quantum_pedestal.md) incantation, which gives the same protection to a Levitite Helmet.

Cracked Veil Masks lie on the floor of some gas caves, a hint of whoever explored them before you.
