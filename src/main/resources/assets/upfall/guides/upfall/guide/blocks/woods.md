# Wood & Canopies

Three tree species grow in the Upfall, each tied to a kind of terrain, and each comes with the full set of wooden building blocks. Next to them, soft Canopy blocks give you colorful cloth-like building material.

## Working with wood

Every set works like vanilla wood:

- **Planks**: one log of the matching species (or its stripped or wood variant, as a log tag) gives 4 planks.
- **Wood**: 2x2 logs give 3 Wood blocks (bark on all six sides); the same works with stripped logs.
- **Stripped**: use an axe on a Log or Wood block.
- **Stairs, slabs, fences, gates, doors, trapdoors, buttons and pressure plates** use the standard recipes with the species planks (stairs 4, slabs 6, doors 3, trapdoors 2, fences 3).
- Wood is flammable, so keep it away from lava.

## Leaves and saplings

- Leaves drop **saplings** and the occasional stick when broken. Shears or Silk Touch keep the leaf block itself. Fortune improves the sapling chance.
- Saplings can be planted on any dirt-type block, including <ItemLink id="skysoil" /> and <ItemLink id="skygrass_block" />, and grow into a tree in time. Light is needed; bone meal speeds it up.
- All three species also grow naturally; see below.

## Skyreach

  <BlockImage id="skyreach_planks" scale="4" />

The common tree of the Upfall, with pale blue wood and cyan leaves. It grows on Skygrass on the top of islands, in the Drifting Archipelago, and more sparsely in the Gilded Ridge, Hanging Roots and Mistsea. A Skyreach sapling planted on dirt grows into the same tree.

## Gildroot

<Row gap="10">
  <BlockImage id="gildroot_planks" scale="4" />
  <BlockImage id="gildroot_leaves" scale="4" />
  <BlockImage id="gildroot_sapling" scale="4" />
</Row>

A golden tree that grows **upside down**, hanging from the underside of islands in the Hanging Roots biome, where gravity is inverted. Its wood is golden and its leaves yellow, and the leaves can drop <ItemLink id="gildfruit" /> when broken without shears.

The Gildroot sapling is special: it hangs from the underside of a block instead of standing on one. Place it against the bottom of Skysoil, Skygrass, Dense Cloud or Duskslate, in a spot with enough light below. It then grows into a hanging tree.

## Canopies

<Row gap="10">
  <BlockImage id="sky_canopy" scale="4" />
  <BlockImage id="dusk_canopy" scale="4" />
  <BlockImage id="pearl_canopy" scale="4" />

Canopies are soft cloth-like blocks used for awnings and roofs, especially in the hamlets of the Loftkin. Each comes as a block, stairs and slab:

| Block | Craft from |
|-------|------------|
| <ItemLink id="sky_canopy" /> | 2x2 Light Blue Wool, gives 4 |
| <ItemLink id="dusk_canopy" /> | 2x2 Pink Wool, gives 4 |
| <ItemLink id="pearl_canopy" /> | 2x2 White Wool, gives 4 |

<RecipeFor id="sky_canopy" />

Stairs (4 from six blocks) and slabs (6 from three) use the usual shapes. Canopies are flammable and soft, so they break quickly.
