# Duskslate

A dense blue-grey slate, tougher than Pearlstone. It makes up the underside of the islands and the bare surface of the Gilded Ridge, and it is where the rarer ores hide.

## Where to find it

- The bottom layer of islands is Duskslate, and the Gilded Ridge biome has it as its surface rock.
- Ores such as Nimbusite and Levitite can be embedded in it; see [Materials & Equipment](../materials/index.md).
- It needs a pickaxe. Mining it drops **Cobbled Duskslate**; use Silk Touch to keep plain Duskslate.

## Crafting

- **Duskslate**: smelt Cobbled Duskslate.
- **Polished Duskslate**: 2x2 of Duskslate, gives 4.
- **Duskslate Bricks**: 2x2 of Polished Duskslate, gives 4.

<RecipeFor id="polished_duskslate" />
<RecipeFor id="duskslate_bricks" />

Cobbled, plain, Polished and Brick Duskslate each have stairs, slabs and walls (4 stairs, 6 slabs or 6 walls per craft). In a Stonecutter, one block gives 1 stair, 1 wall or 2 slabs, and Duskslate can be cut directly into polished, brick and brick-variant forms.

## Gilded Duskslate

<BlockImage id="gilded_duskslate" scale="4" float="right" />

Duskslate shot through with gold. It generates in veins inside Duskslate, in the Gilded Ridge. Mined without Silk Touch it drops Gold Nuggets (Fortune increases the amount). You can also craft it as a golden decorative slate:

<RecipeFor id="gilded_duskslate" />

Two Duskslate and one Gold Nugget make two Gilded Duskslate, so a gilded trim is cheap.
