# Pipe Bridge

<BlockImage id="rusty_pipe" scale="4" float="right" />

Pipe Bridges are giant pipes laid by the old Horologists between the islands of the **Rustworks**. They are not
buildings with rooms or loot; they are a way to cross the gaps between islands without a single block of your own.

<br clear="all" />

## Where to find them

- Only in the **Rustworks** biome (the small, rusty islands under the red fog).
- They are common there: the game tries to place one every few chunks, as soon as two islands face each other
  across a gap.
- A bridge links two edges that are between **8 and 80 blocks** apart. The two ends may differ in height by up to 12 blocks.
  The tube sags slightly in the middle on long spans.
- They keep their distance (about 44 blocks) from a [Stalled Engine](stalled_engine.md).

## What they look like

- A straight tube of <ItemLink id="rusty_plating" /> with a radius of 2 or 3 blocks, sunk three blocks into the rock on both
  ends.
- **Ring flanges** of Rusty Plating and Rustslate wrap the pipe every 6 blocks, and some bridges carry a
  <ItemLink id="pipe_valve" /> on the side (about one in two).
- About **60%** of the pipes are **hollow**: you can walk inside the tube, sheltered from the fall. The rest are solid, and
  you walk on top.

## Dangers

- About one long bridge in four (gaps of 14 or more blocks) has a **broken section**, 2 to 4 blocks long, where the pipe
  is missing and the ends sag. Jump it, bridge it with blocks, or use a glide.
- There is no railing: a walk on top of the pipe above the cloud sea ends badly if you slip. The Nimbusite and Levitite
  armors make this a lot more comfortable.

## Tips

- Inside a hollow pipe you are shielded from the open sky, which makes it a safe way to travel between islands.
- Rusty Plating and Pipe Valves can be mined from the bridge if you need them. See the
  [Structures](index.md) overview for the other places of the Rustworks.
