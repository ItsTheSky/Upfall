## Flicker Shard

<ItemLink id="flicker_shard" /> is harvested from [Flicker Shard Clusters](ores.md), crystals that grow on cave walls. They are only stable while a player is looking at them. If you look away, or start digging while no one watches the crystal, it jumps to another wall in the neighbourhood.

To harvest one:

- Keep the cluster in your line of sight for the whole time you mine it.
- Break it for 1 to 3 shards (Fortune increases this). With Silk Touch you keep the whole cluster instead.
- Have a friend watch it if you need to look elsewhere.

Shards are also found in the chests of gas caves and the Shifting Vault.
