# Advancements

The Upfall adds its own advancement tab, **The Upfall** ("Somewhere above the clouds, the sky falls upward"). It appears once you hold a Halo Frame or a Skyward Key. Advancements marked *goal* or *challenge* use the matching frame. Every branch starts from **Upfall**, and each advancement follows the one it really depends on.

## Getting in

| Advancement | How to get it |
|---|---|
| A Key to the Sky | Craft a Skyward Key from an Elytra, a Nether Star and feathers. |
| Halo of the Heavens (goal) | Light a Halo Portal high in the Overworld sky, above Y 200. |
| Upfall | Step through the Halo Portal into The Upfall. |

## Exploration

| Advancement | How to get it |
|---|---|
| Lifted | Ride a column of Updraft Mist. |
| Following the Currents | Obtain an Updraft Compass (after Lifted). |
| Where Forests Hang | Discover the Hanging Roots. |
| Unbearable Lightness | Become Weightless (after Where Forests Hang). |
| Holding Your Breath | Find a cave filled with Veil Gas; a Veil Mask from the Rustworks lets you breathe there. |
| Don't Blink | Mine a Flicker Shard in a gas cave while keeping an eye on it (after Holding Your Breath). |
| Glittering Depths | Find a Crystal Grotto, a geode hidden under the cloud sea. |
| Sky-Touched | Enchant an item with Skywalker, Uplifted, Cloudsight or Jumpstart. |

## Cloud Hamlet

| Advancement | How to get it |
|---|---|
| A Village in the Clouds | Visit a Cloud Hamlet, hanging above the cloud sea. |
| Rope-craft | Barter with a Loftkin in a Cloud Hamlet. |
| Hold On Tight | Slide along a zipline with a Zipline Handle. |

## Gear

| Advancement | How to get it |
|---|---|
| Cloud Metal | Mine Nimbusite with a netherite pickaxe. |
| Blueprint of the Skies | Find a Nimbusite Upgrade Smithing Template in Drifting Ruins, an Inverted Observatory or a Pearl Aerie, or buy one from a Loftkin. |
| Lighter than Netherite (goal) | Wear a full set of Nimbusite armor; 7 diamonds and a Pearlstone block copy the template. |
| Gilded Heights | Reach the rare Gilded Ridge, the only place where Levitite ore grows (after Blueprint of the Skies). |
| Pieces of Lightness | Mine Levitite ore in the Gilded Ridge; it needs a Nimbusite pickaxe. |
| The Inverted Observatory (goal) | Recover a Levitite Upgrade Smithing Template; every Inverted Observatory, hanging under the Hanging Roots, holds one. |
| Above It All (challenge) | Wear a full set of Levitite armor. |

## Rustworks and machines

| Advancement | How to get it |
|---|---|
| Stalled, Not Dead | Find a Stalled Engine, the ruined pump hall of the Rustworks, whose chests hold machine blueprints. |
| Perfect Timing | Obtain a Kairite Gear by mining Rustworks Kairite ore as it flares, or from nine Kairite Dust. |
| Clockwork | Smith a machine from a Stalled Engine blueprint, its base and a Kairite Gear (after Perfect Timing). |
| Breathe Easy | Smith a Veil Mask from its blueprint, an iron helmet and a Kairite Gear to brave the Veil Gas (after Perfect Timing). |

## Structures

| Advancement | How to get it |
|---|---|
| Keeper of Hours | Find a Chronospire, the floating tower that turns with the days of the Overworld. |
| Borrowed Time (challenge) | Solve the puzzles of a Chronospire and take the Rewinding Hourglass from its vault. |
| City in the Clouds | Find a Pearl Aerie, the abandoned city of the sky builders in the Drifting Archipelago. |
| Sceptre of the Builders (goal) | Recover the Gravity Sceptre from the keep of a Pearl Aerie. |

## Quantum

| Advancement | How to get it |
|---|---|
| Caught Red-Handed | Open the chest of a Shifting Vault and take its Quantum Pedestal Blueprint. |
| Superposition | Smith a Quantum Pedestal from its blueprint, a Pedestal and a Quantum Stone. |
| Spooky Action (goal) | Complete an incantation on a Quantum Pedestal to empower a piece of Levitite gear. |

## Tilted Reliquary

| Advancement | How to get it |
|---|---|
| Tilted Reliquary | Reach the Tilted Reliquary, frozen mid-shatter; its core opens only for four Fold Shards. |
| Folded Space | Find a Fold Shard in a chest of the satellite cubes around a Tilted Reliquary. |
| Unfolded (challenge) | Fill the four Fold Sockets to wake the Fold Warden, then defeat it. |
| Fold Back (goal) | Take the Fold Anchor from the Fold Warden sealed in the heart of a Tilted Reliquary. |
