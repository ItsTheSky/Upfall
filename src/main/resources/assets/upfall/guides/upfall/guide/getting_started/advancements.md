# Advancements

The Upfall adds its own advancement tab, **The Upfall** ("Somewhere above the clouds, the sky falls upward"). It appears once you hold a Halo Frame or a Skyward Key. Advancements marked *goal* or *challenge* use the matching frame.

## Getting in

| Advancement | How to get it |
|---|---|
| A Key to the Sky | Craft a Skyward Key from an Elytra, a Nether Star and feathers. |
| Halo of the Heavens (goal) | Light a Halo Portal high in the Overworld sky, above Y 200. |
| Upfall | Step through the Halo Portal into The Upfall. |

## Exploration

| Advancement | How to get it |
|---|---|
| Where Forests Hang | Discover the Hanging Roots. |
| Unbearable Lightness | Become Weightless (after Where Forests Hang). |
| Gilded Heights | Reach the rare Gilded Ridge (after Lighter than Netherite). |
| Lifted | Ride a column of Updraft Mist. |
| Following the Currents | Obtain an Updraft Compass (after Lifted). |
| Holding Your Breath | Find a cave filled with Veil Gas. |
| Don't Blink | Mine a Flicker Shard while keeping an eye on it. |

## Structures and people

| Advancement | How to get it |
|---|---|
| A Village in the Clouds | Visit a Cloud Hamlet, hanging above the cloud sea. |
| Rope-craft | Barter with a Loftkin. |
| Hold On Tight | Slide along a zipline with a Zipline Handle. |
| Keeper of Hours | Find a Chronospire, the floating tower that turns with the days of the Overworld. |
| Borrowed Time (challenge) | Solve the puzzles of a Chronospire and take the Rewinding Hourglass from its vault. |
| City in the Clouds | Find a Pearl Aerie, the abandoned city of the sky builders. |
| Sceptre of the Builders (goal) | Recover the Gravity Sceptre from the keep of a Pearl Aerie. |
| Stalled, Not Dead | Find a Stalled Engine, the ruined pump hall of the Rustworks. |
| Caught Red-Handed | Open the chest of a Shifting Vault. |

## Gear

| Advancement | How to get it |
|---|---|
| Cloud Metal | Mine Nimbusite with a netherite pickaxe. |
| Blueprint of the Skies | Find a Nimbusite Upgrade Smithing Template in the Drifting Ruins. |
| Lighter than Netherite (goal) | Wear a full set of Nimbusite armor. |
| Pieces of Lightness | Mine Levitite ore with a Nimbusite pickaxe (after Gilded Heights). |
| The Inverted Observatory (goal) | Recover a Levitite Upgrade Smithing Template. |
| Above It All (challenge) | Wear a full set of Levitite armor. |
