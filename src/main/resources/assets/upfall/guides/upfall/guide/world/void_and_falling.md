# Void and Falling

Fall through a hole in the cloud sea or off an island and you drop into the abyss.

If you fall below the bottom of the world, the sky lets you go: you are sent back to the **Overworld**, high in the sky above a random spot nearby, with **Slow Falling** for 30 seconds. Your inventory is kept. Items that fall into the void are lost.

To avoid falling in the first place, use [Dense Cloud, Mist, Skywater and cascades](cloud_sea.md) to cushion falls, and [Updrafts](updrafts_and_cascades.md) to climb back up.
