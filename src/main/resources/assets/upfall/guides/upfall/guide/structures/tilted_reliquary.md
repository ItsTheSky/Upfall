# Tilted Reliquary

<ItemImage id="fold_anchor" scale="4" float="right" />

A massive cluster of **hollow lattice-stone cubes**, each one tilted 45 degrees and caught mid-shatter, with debris
hanging motionless around them. It is the only known source of the <ItemLink id="fold_anchor" />.

<br clear="all" />

## Finding it

- Biomes: Drifting Archipelago, Mistsea and Gilded Ridge.
- Very rare: about one per 2500 by 2500 blocks.
- It stands alone in a cleared stretch of sky above the cloud sea. No islands lie nearby, as if the Fold pushed the land
  away, but the cloud sea still lies below.

## Getting in

- An **updraft column** rises from the cloud sea to the lowest cube. Ride it up.
- Between cubes, use the **Updraft Vents**, **zipline ropes** and **floating <ItemLink id="lattice_beam" />s** used as jump paths.

## The satellite cubes

The small cubes around the core each have a different interior: an **archive**, a **hanging garden**, an **echo hall**
(with a Duskwing spawner), a **collapsed** cube and a **shard vault**.

At least four satellite chests each hold one <ItemLink id="fold_shard" />.

## The core cube

<Row gap="10">
  <BlockImage id="fold_socket" scale="3" />
  <BlockImage id="fold_seal" scale="3" />
</Row>

The heart of the core cube is sealed by **Fold Seal** blocks, which cannot be broken. Outside the seal wall
stand four **Fold Socket** blocks. Right-click each one with a Fold Shard: once all four are filled, the
seals dissolve.

The core chest always holds the <ItemLink id="fold_anchor" /> and the **Warden's note**.

## Lore

Three books are scattered through the cubes: the **Surveyor's journal**, a **letter to the Horologists** and the
**Warden's note**. They tell how the Surveyors of the Fold tried to store the weight of the world in lattice vaults. The
experiment, the Tilt, turned everything 45 degrees and froze it mid-shatter.

## The Fold Anchor

<ItemImage id="fold_anchor" scale="3" float="left" />

The Fold Anchor has **64 durability**.

- **Use** (or **sneak and use**): you set an anchor at your position.
- **Use again** within **30 seconds**, in the same dimension: you snap back to the anchor and your fall distance is reset.
  This costs 1 durability.
- A 5-second cooldown applies after every snap.
- Repair it on an anvil with a <ItemLink id="cloud_pearl" />.

<br clear="all" />

## Tips

- Bring a way to slow your fall. The Fold Anchor is itself an anti-fall tool: set an anchor before a risky jump.
- The cloud sea is the only floor.
