# Gilded Ridge

A harsher, rockier biome of dark Duskslate ridges and sparse vegetation. It is the only biome where <ItemLink id="levitite_ore" /> generates.

## Terrain

Ridges of Duskslate with patches of Skygrass. Few trees and plenty of Gilded Tufts, which give the biome its name. Skywater ponds are small.

## What you find

- **Levitite Ore** and **gilded veins**: the key resource for flying equipment, smelted into <ItemLink id="levitite_ingot" />. See [Materials](../materials/index.md).
- <ItemLink id="nimbusite_ore" /> and <ItemLink id="pearled_cloud" />.
- Sparse Skyreach trees, dense Gilded Tufts, Cloud Ferns and Duskblooms.
- **Structures**: Drifting Ruins.

## Creatures

No peaceful animals here. Cragmaws are the most common; Duskwings appear on the ridges too.

> Tip: bring a way back down before exploring. The ridges are tall and there is little water to break a fall.
