# Cragmaw

<GameScene zoom="2" interactive={true}>
  <Entity id="upfall:cragmaw" />
</GameScene>

The Cragmaw is the main predator of the islands: a heavily armoured, bone-plated brute that charges anything that comes close.

## Where it spawns

- Only in the Upfall, at Y 80 or higher, standing on solid ground. Light level does not matter, so it also hunts in daylight.
- Found in Gilded Ridge (most common), Drifting Archipelago, Hanging Roots and Rustworks. It does not appear in the Mistsea.
- Spawns alone or in pairs.

## Behaviour

- Attacks players on sight within 32 blocks, and retaliates against anything that hurts it.
- Leaps at its target before biting, so it closes distance quickly.
- Also hunts [Skygrazers](skygrazer.md); they run from it when it gets within 12 blocks.

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Health | 44 (22 hearts) |
| Armor | 6 |
| Melee damage | 12 (6 hearts) |
| Knockback resistance | 50% |
| Experience | 12 |

## Drops

- <ItemLink id="cragmaw_fang" />: 1 to 2.
- Bone: 0 to 2.
- Looting adds up to 1 extra of each per level.

The Cragmaw Fang is a trophy-grade drop and is not used in any recipe.

## Tips

- A single Cragmaw can take a big chunk of your health in a few hits. Fight with armor and a shield, or hold a corridor so it can only come at you from one direction.
- Its knockback resistance makes it hard to push off an island, so do not count on edges to get rid of it.
- Keep Skygrazer herds away from Cragmaw territory.
