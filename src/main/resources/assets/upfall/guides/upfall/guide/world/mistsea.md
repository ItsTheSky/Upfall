# Mistsea

The Mistsea is the cloud sea at its thickest: a deep, quiet ocean of <ItemLink id="mist" /> that wraps the base of the islands.

## Terrain

The surface of the cloud band here is **Mist** four blocks deep, instead of two elsewhere, over Dense Cloud. The sea also rises a little higher than in other biomes, with holes opening onto the void. See [The Cloud Sea](cloud_sea.md) for how Mist and Dense Cloud behave.

## What you find

- **Plants**: dense Cloudstalks with Cloudblooms, plus Gilded Tufts and Cloud Ferns.
- **Floating cloudlets**: more common than anywhere else.
- **Raincloud patches** and sparse trees.
- **Ores**: <ItemLink id="pearled_cloud" /> and <ItemLink id="nimbusite_ore" />.
- **Structures**: Drifting Ruins.

## Creatures

Mistjellies and Skyrays are by far the most common here. Hostile mobs are rare: only the odd Duskwing.

> Tip: Mist slows you down and whitens your view. Crossing the Mistsea on foot is easy to do and easy to get lost in; follow an Updraft or keep to the high ground.
