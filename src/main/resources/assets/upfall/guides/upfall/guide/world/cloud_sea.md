# The Cloud Sea

Between the islands and the abyss lies a thick band of clouds, with holes that open onto the void. You can walk, bounce and hide in it.

## Dense Cloud

<Row gap="10">
  <BlockImage id="dense_cloud" scale="3" />
  <ItemImage id="dense_cloud" scale="2" />
</Row>

<ItemLink id="dense_cloud" /> is the solid body of the sea. It is a full block you can stand on, and it is **bouncy**: a hard fall onto it is cushioned (fall damage is halved) and you rebound. Sneak to land without bouncing.

## Mist

<Row gap="10">
  <BlockImage id="mist" scale="3" />
  <ItemImage id="mist" scale="2" />
</Row>

<ItemLink id="mist" /> forms the top of the sea: two blocks deep, four in the [Mistsea](mistsea.md). It has no collision, so you sink into it.

- It **slows** your movement.
- It **softens a fall**: falling into Mist cancels fall damage unless you plunge very fast.
- Inside it, your view whitens to fog.

Some equipment changes this: enchantments and Quantum upgrades can reduce the drag or let you bounce on Mist. See [Materials](../materials/index.md) and [Machines & Quantum](../machines/index.md).

## Fading Cloud

<Row gap="10">
  <BlockImage id="fading_cloud" scale="3" />
</Row>

**Fading Cloud** is a temporary Dense Cloud. It behaves like one, but after about 30 seconds it puffs away. The <ItemLink id="nimbus_flask" /> places them, giving you a short-lived bridge across the void.

## Raincloud

<Row gap="10">
  <BlockImage id="raincloud" scale="3" />
  <ItemImage id="raincloud" scale="2" />
</Row>

<ItemLink id="raincloud" /> is Dense Cloud that drips. Raincloud patches float in the sea; they are scenery, as bouncy as ordinary clouds.

## Cloudlets and flora

Small floating cloudlets drift above the sea, especially in the Mistsea. Cloudstalks and Cloudblooms grow on the clouds. <ItemLink id="pearled_cloud" /> is a rarer, valuable cloud block found embedded in the sea; see [Materials](../materials/index.md).

## The abyss and the needles

Below the sea there is only void. Rare **stone needles** rise from the abyss: tall spires of Pearlstone and Duskslate with glowing tips. The Crystal Grotto also hangs below the sea; see [Structures](../structures/index.md).

See [Void and Falling](void_and_falling.md) for what happens if you fall.
