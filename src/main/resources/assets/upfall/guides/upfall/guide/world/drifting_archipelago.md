# Drifting Archipelago

The default biome of the Upfall: grassy islands of every size drifting in the golden light, with small lakes of Skywater and the occasional ruin.

## Terrain

Floating islands of Skygrass over Skysoil, with Pearlstone and Duskslate underneath. Skywater streams, lakes and ponds fill the hollows. Upfall Cascades and Updraft columns connect the layers.

## What you find

- **Trees and plants**: Skyreach trees, Gilded Tufts, Cloud Ferns, Duskblooms and Cloudstalks.
- **Ores**: <ItemLink id="nimbusite_ore" /> and <ItemLink id="pearled_cloud" />. See [Materials](../materials/index.md).
- **Crystals and vines**: Mistglow Crystals and Glowvines.
- **Water features**: [Upfall Cascades](updrafts_and_cascades.md), Skywater lakes and ponds.
- **Structures**: Drifting Ruins. See [Structures](../structures/index.md).

## Creatures

Skygrazers graze on the grass, Skyrays glide overhead and Mistjellies float in the water. Hostile Cragmaws and rare Duskwings roam the islands.
