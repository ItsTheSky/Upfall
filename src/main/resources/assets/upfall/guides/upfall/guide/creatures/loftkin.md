# Loftkin

<GameScene zoom="3" interactive={true}>
  <Entity id="upfall:loftkin" />
</GameScene>

Loftkin are the small, friendly folk of the Upfall, living in Cloud Hamlets and trading ropes, lanterns and sky-travel gear. There are three visual variants, picked at random.

## Life in a Cloud Hamlet

- They live in the [Cloud Hamlet](../structures/cloud_hamlet.md) structure and stay near where they spawned; if they stray more than 30 blocks, or fall far below, they return home by themselves.
- They wave at nearby visitors and wander carefully along platforms, avoiding edges.
- They open doors and do not breed.
- When falling fast, a Loftkin opens its parasol to drift down slowly and takes no fall damage.
- Their trades refill once per in-game day.

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Health | 24 (12 hearts) |
| Knockback resistance | 50% |

### Highlights

- Every Loftkin sells a Nimbusite Upgrade Smithing Template, once: one of the best reasons to find a hamlet.
- Explorer maps are the easiest way to find the rare structures and the Gilded Ridge; see [Progression](../getting_started/progression.md).
- Levitite Shards convert at a profit into Nimbusite Ingots.
- The Updraft Compass points to the nearest Updraft Vent.

## Tips

- Check every Loftkin in a hamlet: the random trades and maps are rolled per individual, so another one may hold the trade you need.
- Bring Compasses if you want maps.
- Cloudberries are the universal currency; bring a stack.
