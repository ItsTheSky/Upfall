# Enchantments

Upfall adds four armor enchantments built around movement through Mist and updrafts. All of them can be found in enchanting tables, in loot chests and in librarian trades, and combined at an anvil as usual.

| Enchantment | Slot | Max level |
|---|---|---|
| Cloudsight | Helmet | I |
| Skywalker | Boots | III |
| Uplifted | Chestplate | III |
| Jumpstart | Levitite Boots only | III |

## Cloudsight

Mist normally blinds you and slows you down. With Cloudsight on your helmet, you see clearly through Mist and move through it at full speed.

## Skywalker

Boots enchantment. Each level cancels about a third of the Mist slowdown (level III removes it entirely) and adds a +45% movement speed bonus while you stand inside Mist.

## Uplifted

Chestplate enchantment. Updraft Mist columns carry you higher and faster. Each level raises both how fast you accelerate upward and your maximum upward speed in an updraft.

## Jumpstart

Only applicable to <ItemLink id="levitite_boots" />. Each level adds jump height and gives a forward push at the start of every jump. Combined with the Levitite [double jump](levitite.md), it makes crossing gaps between islands easy.

> Tip: Cloudsight and Skywalker together let you wade through the Mist sea as if it were air.
