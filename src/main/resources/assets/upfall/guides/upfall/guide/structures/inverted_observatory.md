# Inverted Observatory

![Hanging roots ceiling](../assets/ceiling.png)

A dusk-coloured sphere that hangs from the ceiling of the Hanging Roots, reached by a long shaft. Astronomers once watched the cloud sea from here, upside down. It holds two Sky Caches and one of the best early rewards in the mod.

## Where to find it

- Biome: Hanging Roots, on the underside of the upper islands.
- About one attempt per 14 chunks, so roughly one every few hundred blocks when you fly along the ceiling.
- Look for a 3x3 pillar of <ItemLink id="polished_duskslate" /> with a <ItemLink id="gilded_duskslate" /> band every third block, poking out of the ceiling and going down 4 to 7 blocks. The observatory itself is hidden below it.

## Layout

- A shaft of polished and gilded duskslate leads down to a sphere of 6 blocks radius.
- The shell is <ItemLink id="duskslate_bricks" /> with a gilded belt. Around the equator, "windows" of <ItemLink id="mistglow_crystal" /> let the light in.
- Inside, the floor is polished pearlstone. A <ItemLink id="nimbusite_block" /> stands in the centre under a short gilded pillar.
- Two opposite doorways are open on the sphere's equator; a few roots and vines hang off the bottom.
- The two **Sky Caches** are on the floor, one on each side, left and right of the doorways.

If you cannot see the entrance, follow the shaft downward.

## Loot

Every Sky Cache of an observatory is rolled separately, and each holds 4 to 7 stacks plus guaranteed extras:

- **Guaranteed**: one <ItemLink id="levitite_upgrade_smithing_template" /> per cache.
- Common: <ItemLink id="nimbusite_ingot" />, <ItemLink id="gildfruit" />, gold ingots, <ItemLink id="mistglow_crystal" />, <ItemLink id="cloudberry_tart" />, <ItemLink id="levitite_shard" />.
- Rare: <ItemLink id="updraft_compass" />, <ItemLink id="nimbusite_upgrade_smithing_template" />, diamonds, echo shards.
- 15 percent: a Tilted Reliquary Map.

## Tips

- The sphere hangs far from solid ground: bring blocks to bridge and a way to catch your fall.
- Bring a pickaxe: the shaft walls can be mined to make a safe return path.
- The levitite template is the key to levitite gear; see [Materials & Equipment](../materials/index.md).
