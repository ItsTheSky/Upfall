# Progression

The route from your first portal to the last boss, in the order things really depend on each other. Each tier lists what you need, where to get it, what it unlocks and the advancement that marks it. The **The Upfall** advancement tab follows the same branches; see [Advancements](advancements.md).

The spine is **Arrival, Nimbusite, Levitite, Quantum Pedestal, Tilted Reliquary**. The Rustworks, the gas caves and the big structures are side lines you can tackle in parallel once you have arrived.

## Finding structures: explorer maps

Most structures are rare and far apart. Instead of wandering, buy a map. Every [Loftkin](../creatures/loftkin.md) sells **1 or 2 explorer maps**, picked at random; each trade also costs a vanilla <ItemLink id="minecraft:compass" />. A map marks the nearest target, like a vanilla explorer map.

| Map | Loftkin price (plus a Compass) | Also found in |
|---|---|---|
| Stalled Engine Map | 3 <ItemLink id="gildfruit" /> | Gas cave supply chests (10%) |
| Pearl Aerie Map | 3 <ItemLink id="nimbusite_ingot" /> | Drifting Ruins (5%), Cloud Hamlet homes (8%) |
| Chronospire Map | 3 <ItemLink id="nimbusite_ingot" /> | Drifting Ruins (5%) |
| Shifting Vault Map | 6 <ItemLink id="raw_nimbusite" /> | Stalled Engine hall caches (12%) |
| Gilded Ridge Map | 6 <ItemLink id="gildfruit" /> | - |
| Tilted Reliquary Map | 3 <ItemLink id="levitite_shard" /> | Inverted Observatory (15%), Chronospire vault (25%), Pearl Aerie keep (25%) |

Check every Loftkin of a hamlet: each one rolls its own maps.

## Where to find the bottlenecks

| You need | Where to get it |
|---|---|
| <ItemLink id="nimbusite_upgrade_smithing_template" /> | Pearl Aerie keep and armories (best odds), Inverted Observatory, Drifting Ruins (25%), any Loftkin (once, 10 Nimbusite Ingots + 4 Gildfruit). Copy it with 7 diamonds and a pearlstone block. |
| <ItemLink id="levitite_ore" /> | Only in the rare [Gilded Ridge](../world/gilded_ridge.md); needs a Nimbusite pickaxe. |
| <ItemLink id="levitite_upgrade_smithing_template" /> | [Inverted Observatory](../structures/inverted_observatory.md) (one per cache, guaranteed), Chronospire vault, Pearl Aerie keep, Tilted Reliquary core. Copy it with 7 Nimbusite Ingots and a duskslate block. |
| Machine blueprints | [Stalled Engine](../structures/stalled_engine.md) vault and hall caches, Stalled Automatons of the Rustworks. |
| <ItemLink id="veil_mask_blueprint" /> | Stalled Engine hall caches (the most common blueprint there). |
| <ItemLink id="quantum_pedestal_blueprint" /> | Always in the chest of a [Shifting Vault](../structures/shifting_vault.md). Copy blueprints with 7 Kairite Dust and a rustslate. |
| <ItemLink id="fold_shard" /> | Satellite cube chests around a [Tilted Reliquary](../structures/tilted_reliquary.md); you need four. |

## 1. Arrival

- **Need**: a Nether Star, an Elytra and netherite to craft <ItemLink id="halo_frame" /> blocks and a <ItemLink id="skyward_key" />. Light the portal above Y 200.
- **Then**: settle on your arrival island, set a bed, find a [Cloud Hamlet](../structures/cloud_hamlet.md) and trade with the Loftkin for ziplines, ropes and maps.
- **Read**: [Reaching the Upfall](reaching_the_upfall.md), [First Steps](first_steps.md).
- **Advancements**: A Key to the Sky, Halo of the Heavens, Upfall; then A Village in the Clouds and Rope-craft.

## 2. Nimbusite

- **Need**: a **netherite pickaxe** to mine <ItemLink id="raw_nimbusite" />, smelted into <ItemLink id="nimbusite_ingot" />, and a <ItemLink id="nimbusite_upgrade_smithing_template" /> (see the table above).
- **Unlocks**: Nimbusite tools and armor, smithed from netherite. The full set cuts fall damage to a quarter, and the **Nimbusite pickaxe** is the only way to mine Levitite.
- **Read**: [Nimbusite](../materials/nimbusite.md).
- **Advancements**: Cloud Metal, Blueprint of the Skies, Lighter than Netherite.

## 3. Side lines

These run in parallel: start them as soon as you arrive, but the Quantum Pedestal needs the Rustworks line, so do not skip it.

### Rustworks, Kairite and the Veil Mask

- **Need**: the [Rustworks](../world/rustworks.md) biome, the only home of <ItemLink id="kairite_ore" /> (an iron pickaxe is enough). Mine it as it flares for a <ItemLink id="kairite_gear" />, or compress nine <ItemLink id="kairite_dust" />.
- **Where**: the Rustworks [Stalled Engine](../structures/stalled_engine.md) holds the machine blueprints and the Veil Mask Blueprint. Buy a Stalled Engine Map from a Loftkin to find one.
- **Unlocks**: clockwork machines (see [Machines & Quantum](../machines/index.md)) and the <ItemLink id="veil_mask" />, which lets you breathe in Veil Gas.
- **Advancements**: Stalled, Not Dead, Perfect Timing, Clockwork, Breathe Easy.

### Gas caves

- **Need**: a Veil Mask, or a lot of care. See [Gas Caves](../world/gas_caves.md).
- **Where**: winding tunnels inside the islands. Supply chests hold <ItemLink id="flicker_shard" />, Kairite Dust and <ItemLink id="bottled_veil_gas" />, and the clusters on the walls give Flicker Shards for the Quantum Stone and incantations.
- **Advancements**: Holding Your Breath, Don't Blink.

### Structures

- [Chronospire](../structures/chronospire.md): solve its puzzles for the <ItemLink id="rewinding_hourglass" />. The vault also holds Levitite shards and ingots and often a Levitite template, so it helps the next tier. Advancements: Keeper of Hours, Borrowed Time.
- [Pearl Aerie](../structures/pearl_aerie.md): the keep holds the <ItemLink id="gravity_sceptre" />, and its chests are the best source of Nimbusite templates. Advancements: City in the Clouds, Sceptre of the Builders.
- [Crystal Grotto](../structures/crystal_grotto.md): a geode under the cloud sea. Advancement: Glittering Depths.

### Exploration

Updrafts, the Hanging Roots and the sky enchantments each have their own advancements: Lifted, Following the Currents, Where Forests Hang, Unbearable Lightness and Sky-Touched. See [The Upfall](../world/index.md) and [Enchantments](../materials/enchantments.md).

## 4. Levitite

- **Need**: a Nimbusite pickaxe and the rare [Gilded Ridge](../world/gilded_ridge.md) biome (a Gilded Ridge Map from a Loftkin saves a long search). Levitite ore drops <ItemLink id="levitite_shard" />; 4 shards and 4 Nimbusite Ingots make one <ItemLink id="levitite_ingot" />.
- **Template**: every [Inverted Observatory](../structures/inverted_observatory.md), hanging under the inverted islands of the Hanging Roots, holds one per cache.
- **Unlocks**: Levitite gear, smithed from Nimbusite. The full set cancels fall damage and adds a double jump and a glide. Every quantum incantation targets Levitite gear.
- **Read**: [Levitite](../materials/levitite.md).
- **Advancements**: Gilded Heights, Pieces of Lightness, The Inverted Observatory, Above It All.

## 5. Quantum Pedestal

- **Need**: Levitite gear to empower, the Kairite line, and a [Shifting Vault](../structures/shifting_vault.md) (bring a Veil Mask: the vault is full of gas). Its chest always holds the <ItemLink id="quantum_pedestal_blueprint" /> and Quantum Stones.
- **Build**: smith a <ItemLink id="pedestal" />, a <ItemLink id="quantum_stone" /> and the blueprint into a <ItemLink id="quantum_pedestal" />, then ring it with eight Pedestals.
- **Unlocks**: incantations that add permanent upgrades to Levitite gear. See [Quantum Pedestal](../machines/quantum_pedestal.md).
- **Advancements**: Caught Red-Handed, Superposition, Spooky Action.

## 6. Finale: the Tilted Reliquary

- **Need**: your best gear and a way to find the structure: it is very rare, about one per 2500 by 2500 blocks. Use a Tilted Reliquary Map (Loftkin, Inverted Observatory, Chronospire vault, Pearl Aerie keep).
- **Do**: loot four <ItemLink id="fold_shard" /> from the satellite cubes, fill the four Fold Sockets of the core and defeat the [Fold Warden](../creatures/fold_warden.md), a two-phase boss.
- **Rewards**: the <ItemLink id="fold_anchor" />, a <ItemLink id="fold_heart" /> trophy and the core treasure. See [Tilted Reliquary](../structures/tilted_reliquary.md).
- **Advancements**: Tilted Reliquary, Folded Space, Unfolded, Fold Back.
