# Progression

A suggested route from your first portal to the last upgrades. The in-game **The Upfall** advancement tab follows much of the same path (see [Advancements](advancements.md)).

## 1. Open the door

Craft <ItemLink id="halo_frame" /> and a <ItemLink id="skyward_key" />, climb above Y 200 and light a portal. See [Reaching the Upfall](reaching_the_upfall.md).

## 2. Survive and explore the archipelago

Settle on your arrival island, set a bed, and explore. [The Upfall](../world/index.md) covers biomes, the cloud sea and gravity; [Creatures](../creatures/index.md) tells you what to fear. Visit a Cloud Hamlet and trade with the Loftkin.

## 3. Nimbusite tier

- Mine <ItemLink id="raw_nimbusite" /> with a **netherite pickaxe** and smelt it into <ItemLink id="nimbusite_ingot" />.
- Find a <ItemLink id="nimbusite_upgrade_smithing_template" /> in the Drifting Ruins; see [Structures](../structures/index.md).
- Smith Nimbusite gear from your netherite pieces; see [Materials & Equipment](../materials/index.md).

## 4. Levitite tier

- Reach the rare **Gilded Ridge** biome, the only home of Levitite Ore. It needs a **Nimbusite pickaxe** and drops <ItemLink id="levitite_shard" />.
- Combine shards with Nimbusite into a <ItemLink id="levitite_ingot" />.
- Recover a <ItemLink id="levitite_upgrade_smithing_template" /> from an Inverted Observatory.
- A full Levitite set cancels fall damage and adds a double jump and a glide; see [Materials & Equipment](../materials/index.md).

## 5. Structures and rewards

- The **Chronospire**: solve its puzzles to claim the <ItemLink id="rewinding_hourglass" />.
- The **Pearl Aerie**, an abandoned sky city: its keep holds the <ItemLink id="gravity_sceptre" />.
- The **Shifting Vault** and its <ItemLink id="shifting_chest" />.
- Details for all of them: [Structures](../structures/index.md).

## 6. Rustworks, Kairite and machines

- The **Rustworks** biome hides the **Stalled Engine**, a ruined pump hall; see [The Upfall](../world/index.md).
- Mine Kairite there and craft <ItemLink id="kairite_gear" />.
- Kairite Gears are the key ingredient of the machine blueprints (smithing templates), which unlock the clockwork machines, pneumatic pipes and more; see [Machines & Quantum](../machines/index.md).

## 7. The Quantum Pedestal

The <ItemLink id="quantum_pedestal" /> lets you complete incantations and apply upgrades such as the Void Tether. It is the last step of the machine line; see [Machines & Quantum](../machines/index.md).

## Building the base

All along, expand and decorate with the Pearlstone, Duskslate, Skyreach and Gildroot families: [Blocks & Building](../blocks/index.md).
