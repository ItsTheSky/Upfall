# Soils & Plants

The Upfall has its own soils and its own flora. Most plants refuse to grow on vanilla grass, so knowing what each one needs is the key to a garden.

## Soils

### Skygrass Block and Skysoil

<BlockImage id="skygrass_block" scale="4" float="right" />

**Skysoil** is the dirt of the Upfall. **Skygrass** is Skysoil with a living top, and the surface of most islands. Both count as dirt, so saplings can be planted in them.

Skygrass can grow **upside down**. When you place a Skygrass Block against the *underside* of a block (clicking the bottom face), it becomes an inverted block with its grass on the bottom, as seen on the ceilings of the Hanging Roots biome. In both orientations:

- Grass spreads onto nearby Skysoil as long as the grass face is open to the light.
- Grass reverts to Skysoil if it gets covered by an opaque block.
- Breaking it gives Skysoil; use Silk Touch to keep the grass.
- **Bone meal** scatters plants on the grass face of nearby Skygrass: Gilded Tufts, Cloud Ferns and Duskblooms on regular grass, and Glowvines on inverted grass.
<br clear="all" />

Skygrass is the only soil on which <ItemLink id="skyreach_sapling" /> trees spawn naturally. Skygrazers and other grazing creatures prefer it too.

### Skysilt

<BlockImage id="skysilt" scale="4" float="right" />

Soft pale silt that forms the bed of the sky lakes and ponds. It is plantable (water plants like <ItemLink id="skyreed" /> root in it), and it can be smelted into regular **Glass**.
<br clear="all" />

### Oxide Turf and Rustsoil

<BlockImage id="oxide_turf" scale="4" float="right" />

The Rustworks equivalent of grass and dirt. **Oxide Turf** covers the surface of the biome, with a couple of layers of **Rustsoil** underneath. Turf spreads onto nearby Rustsoil when it has light, and decays into Rustsoil when covered. Rivetgrass, Verdigris Bloom and Ironbark trees all grow on Oxide Turf. Below that it is [Rustslate](rustslate.md).
<br clear="all" />

## Plants of the islands

Most of these are placed on soil of the Upfall: Skygrass, Skysoil, Dense Cloud, Duskslate and anything else tagged as dirt.

| Plant | Where and how |
|-------|---------------|
| <ItemLink id="gilded_tuft" /> | Short golden grass that grows in patches on Skygrass, thickest in the Gilded Ridge. Shears pick it up. |
| <ItemLink id="cloud_fern" /> | Cyan fern in patches on islands, also in the Rustworks. Shears pick it up; breaking it by hand can also drop Cloudberries. Can be potted. |
| <ItemLink id="duskbloom" /> | A pink flower. Used in suspicious stew to give Slow Falling. Can be potted. |
| <ItemLink id="glowvine" /> | A glowing vine that hangs from the underside of islands and spreads downward over time. It is climbable and lights its surroundings. Bone meal grows it; shears or Silk Touch keep it. |

## Plants of the clouds and lakes

| Plant | Where and how |
|-------|---------------|
| <ItemLink id="cloudstalk" /> | A pale pillar that grows on top of Dense Cloud, Pearled Cloud or Raincloud, and stacks upward. Found in patches, denser over the Mistsea. |
| <ItemLink id="cloudbloom" /> | A glowing flower that crowns a Cloudstalk (it can sit on a cloud or on a stalk). It gives light and releases small motes. It pops off if its support is removed. |
| <ItemLink id="skylily" /> | A lily that floats on Skywater, glowing softly. Place it on the surface of Skywater. |
| <ItemLink id="skyreed" /> | A tall two-block plant growing at the edge of sky lakes, on soil or silt. |

## Quick tips

- Use **shears** on grass, ferns, tufts and vines to get the plant itself.
- Use **bone meal** on Skygrass to seed a garden; stand on the grass side that is open to the air.
- Light matters: grass and turf beneath a solid block turn back into plain soil.
- Trees are covered in [Wood & Canopies](woods.md); glowing plants are also listed in [Lights](lights.md).
