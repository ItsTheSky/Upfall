# Drifting Ruins

![The Upfall skyline](../assets/vista.png)

Small circles of broken pearlstone floating on the grassy islands of the Upfall. They are the first structures most travellers find: a crumbled floor, a ring of stubby pillars and, in the middle, a single container waiting on a pedestal.

## Where to find them

- Biomes: Drifting Archipelago, Gilded Ridge and Mistsea.
- They only appear on top of skygrass, on islands roughly 64 blocks and higher.
- Frequent: about one attempt per 32 chunks, so you usually spot several while crossing an archipelago.

A ruin is a disc of mixed pearlstone bricks, cobbled pearlstone and polished pearlstone, 3 to 4 blocks in radius, with a few holes in the floor. Four to six pillars of <ItemLink id="pearlstone_bricks" /> of uneven height stand around the edge; some have a floating brick or slab above them and a fallen stair beside them.

At the exact centre, on a polished pearlstone pedestal, sits a **Sky Cache**.

## The Sky Cache

<ItemImage id="sky_cache" scale="3" float="right" />

The Sky Cache is a 27-slot container, like a chest, that only generates in structures. Open it with right click. It also works as a storage block: it can be broken and picked up, and it outputs a comparator signal.

### Ruins loot

Each ruin cache holds 3 to 6 stacks picked from:

- Food: <ItemLink id="gildfruit" />, <ItemLink id="cloudberry" />, and sometimes a <ItemLink id="cloudberry_tart" />
- <ItemLink id="raw_nimbusite" /> and a few <ItemLink id="nimbusite_ingot" />
- <ItemLink id="skyreach_sapling" />, <ItemLink id="mistglow_crystal" /> and <ItemLink id="dense_cloud" />
- Gold nuggets and feathers

There is also a 25 percent chance of a <ItemLink id="nimbusite_upgrade_smithing_template" />, and a small chance (5 percent each) of a Chronospire Map or a Pearl Aerie Map.

## Tips

- The edge of the disc is ragged and has holes: watch your step.
- Ruins are a good early source of saplings, food and a first template. For more templates, raid a [Pearl Aerie](pearl_aerie.md) or buy one from a [Loftkin](../creatures/loftkin.md).
