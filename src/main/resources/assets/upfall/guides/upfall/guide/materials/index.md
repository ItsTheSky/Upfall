# Materials & Equipment

The Upfall has its own metals, crystals and curios. This chapter covers what to mine, what to forge, and what to wear or carry.

## Progression at a glance

1. Find [Nimbusite ore](ores.md) and smelt it. You need a netherite-tier pickaxe to mine it.
2. Recover a Nimbusite Upgrade Smithing Template from a structure and forge [Nimbusite equipment](nimbusite.md) from netherite gear.
3. Mine [Levitite ore](ores.md) in the Gilded Ridge with a Nimbusite pickaxe and forge [Levitite equipment](levitite.md): double jump, glide and no fall damage.
4. Along the way, collect [Kairite](kairite.md), [quantum materials](quantum_materials.md), [food](food.md) and [gadgets](gadgets.md), and enchant your gear with the [Upfall enchantments](enchantments.md).
