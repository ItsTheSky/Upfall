# First Steps

You step out of the portal onto an island of the **Drifting Archipelago**. Here is what to know before wandering off.

## What to bring

- A **netherite pickaxe**: <ItemLink id="raw_nimbusite" /> cannot be mined with anything lesser.
- Netherite armor, plenty of food and a good weapon; see [Creatures](../creatures/index.md).
- Building blocks, a bed and torches.
- Elytra and Firework Rockets are handy for crossing gaps, and a <ItemLink id="nimbus_flask" /> makes an emergency platform when you fall.

## Dangers

- **The cloud sea and the abyss.** Under the islands lies a sea of condensed cloud. Dense Cloud can be walked on; Mist blinds and slows you. Below the sea is the abyss.
- **Fall damage.** Islands are high. Nimbusite and Levitite armor blunt or cancel it.
- **Predators.** Several creatures hunt on the islands and in the sky; see [Creatures](../creatures/index.md).
- **Gas caves.** Some caves are filled with Veil Gas; see [The Upfall](../world/index.md).

## The sky lets you go

Falling out of the bottom of the world is **not death**. The sky returns you to the Overworld at a random spot within a few hundred blocks of where you fell, high above the ground, with **Slow Falling for 30 seconds**. You keep your inventory but lose your place in the Upfall.

Glide down, then light a new portal if you still have a Skyward Key and Halo Frames.

## Sleeping and time

The Upfall is locked in its golden hour. Beds work there at **any time of day**, so a bed is a good way to set a spawn point on your first island.

## Getting around

- Learn where gravity bends: some islands hang upside down and flip your gravity beneath them. See [The Upfall](../world/index.md).
- Ride **updraft mist** columns up to higher islands. The <ItemLink id="updraft_compass" /> points to the nearest updraft vent, and the <ItemLink id="updraft_vent" /> lets you make your own.
- Cloud Hamlets are linked by rope bridges and ziplines; hang on with a <ItemLink id="zipline_handle" />.
- Bridge gaps with whatever you like; see [Blocks & Building](../blocks/index.md).
