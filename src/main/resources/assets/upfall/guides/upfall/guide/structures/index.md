# Structures

The Upfall hides ruined towers, floating villages and buried vaults among its islands and caves. Most of them hold chests with rewards you cannot get anywhere else: smithing templates, blueprints, rare ingots and unique tools.

Distances below are the structure spacing: roughly one structure per square of that many chunks (16 blocks each). Smaller means more common.

| Structure | Biome | Frequency | Main reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Drifting Ruins](drifting_ruins.md) | Drifting Archipelago, Gilded Ridge, Mistsea | Small and common: about 1 in 32 chunks | A <ItemLink id="sky_cache" /> with food, nimbusite and saplings |
| [Inverted Observatory](inverted_observatory.md) | Hanging Roots | About 1 in 14 chunks on the ceiling | Levitite upgrade template, diamonds, echo shards |
| [Chronospire](chronospire.md) | Drifting Archipelago, Mistsea | Rare, spacing 80 chunks | <ItemLink id="rewinding_hourglass" /> and a rich vault |
| [Cloud Hamlet](cloud_hamlet.md) | Mistsea, Drifting Archipelago (rarer) | Spacing 28 chunks | <ItemLink id="zipline_handle" />, ropes, trades with Loftkin |
| [Crystal Grotto](crystal_grotto.md) | All biomes, underground | Uncommon, spacing 59 chunks | Crystal clusters, levitite, echo shards |
| [Pipe Bridge](pipe_bridge.md) | Rustworks | Very common, spacing 4 chunks | A route across the Rustworks |
| [Stalled Engine](stalled_engine.md) | Rustworks | Spacing 12 chunks | Machine blueprints, Kairite |
| [Shifting Vault](shifting_vault.md) | Drifting Archipelago, Hanging Roots, Gilded Ridge, Rustworks, underground | Spacing set in the mod config | Quantum Stone, Quantum Pedestal Blueprint |

## Tips for explorers

- Bring blocks to bridge gaps, a way to slow your fall and food: the cloud sea is the only floor under most structures.
- Structure chests do not refill. Take everything.
- Some doors and walls in the Chronospire and other advanced structures are warded: they cannot be broken and only open through their puzzle.
