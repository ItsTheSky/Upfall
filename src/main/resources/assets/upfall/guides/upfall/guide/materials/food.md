# Food

Three foods grow or bake in the Upfall. Mobs have their own dishes; see [Creatures](../creatures/index.md) for meat and jelly.

<Row gap="10">
  <ItemImage id="cloudberry" scale="3" />
  <ItemImage id="cloudberry_tart" scale="3" />
  <ItemImage id="gildfruit" scale="3" />
</Row>

| Food | Hunger restored | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cloudberry | 1 shank | Eaten very quickly |
| Cloudberry Tart | 4 shanks | High saturation |
| Gildfruit | 2 shanks | 35% chance of the Weightless effect |

## Cloudberry

<ItemLink id="cloudberry" /> is picked from Cloud Fern: break the plant without shears and it has a 30% chance to drop a berry (Fortune helps). Eating one is almost instant, which makes it a handy snack in a fight. Skygrazers love them, and Loftkin traders buy and sell them.

## Cloudberry Tart

<ItemLink id="cloudberry_tart" /> is the best prepared food of the Upfall.

<RecipeFor id="cloudberry_tart" />

You will also find tarts in the chests of the Cloud Hamlet, Pearl Aerie, Crystal Grotto and Chronospire.

## Gildfruit

<ItemLink id="gildfruit" /> falls rarely from Gildroot Leaves in the Gilded Ridge (leaves broken without shears). It restores some hunger and has a 35% chance of granting about 8 seconds of **Weightless**, which lowers gravity. See [The Upfall](../world/index.md) for how Weightless works.

Gildfruit is also common in structure chests and can be bought from Loftkin traders.
