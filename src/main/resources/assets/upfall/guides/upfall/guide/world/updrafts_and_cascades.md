# Updrafts and Cascades

The Upfall moves you upward with its own plumbing: columns of rising air and water that flows up.

## Updraft Vent

<Row gap="10">
  <BlockImage id="updraft_vent" scale="3" />
  <ItemImage id="updraft_vent" scale="2" />
</Row>

An <ItemLink id="updraft_vent" /> grows a column of **Updraft Mist** straight up, up to 48 blocks, stopping at the first solid block. Vents appear naturally in the clouds and on islands. The <ItemLink id="updraft_compass" /> points to the nearest one.

## Updraft Mist

Updraft Mist is the glowing column itself. It has no item and cannot be placed. Standing in it pushes you steadily upward and cancels fall damage. Sneak to rise no more and sink slowly instead. The Uplifted enchantment makes you rise faster. If the vent or the column below is broken, the column vanishes.

## Upfall Cascade

<BlockImage id="upfall_cascade" scale="3" />

An **Upfall Cascade** is a waterfall that rises toward the underside of an island. It has no item. Inside one you fall very slowly, take no fall damage, and fire is put out. Cascades are common in [Hanging Roots](hanging_roots.md).

## Skywater

<ItemImage id="skywater_bucket" scale="2" />

**Skywater** is the water of the Upfall, found in the lakes, ponds and streams of the islands. It behaves like ordinary water: you can swim in it, it puts out fire, it cancels fall damage and it forms infinite sources. Scoop it with a bucket to get a <ItemLink id="skywater_bucket" />.

## Travelling

1. Find a Vent (the compass helps), step in and ride up to the next layer.
2. Use cascades to reach the undersides of inverted islands.
3. Come down by falling into Dense Cloud, Mist, Skywater or a cascade.
