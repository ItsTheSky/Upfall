# Lights

The Upfall has no shortage of glow: crystals in the caves, lamps made of cloud, and plants that shine on their own. Here are the ones you can place.

## Cloud Lamp

<BlockImage id="cloud_lamp" scale="3" float="right" />

A block of Dense Cloud lit from within by Cloud Pearls. It gives full brightness (light level 15) and works as a clean, bright building block.

<RecipeFor id="cloud_lamp" />

You need one <ItemLink id="dense_cloud" /> and four <ItemLink id="cloud_pearl" />.
<br clear="all" />

## Mistglow Crystal

<BlockImage id="mistglow_crystal" scale="3" float="right" />

A pale blue crystal block that gives full brightness. Natural Mistglow Crystals hang from the ceilings of caves and the undersides of islands in every biome of the Upfall. Mining one with a pickaxe drops 3 to 4 **Mistglow Dust**; Silk Touch keeps the block.

You can rebuild it from the dust:

<RecipeFor id="mistglow_crystal" />

Mistglow Dust is also an ingredient for the Mistglow Lantern, below.
<br clear="all" />

## Lanterns

<Row gap="10">
  <BlockImage id="cloud_lantern" scale="4" />
  <BlockImage id="mistglow_lantern" scale="4" />
</Row>

Two lanterns give full brightness (light level 15) and can be set on the ground or hung from above, like vanilla lanterns.

- <ItemLink id="cloud_lantern" />: eight Gold Nuggets around a Mistglow Crystal.

<RecipeFor id="cloud_lantern" />

- <ItemLink id="mistglow_lantern" />: four Gold Nuggets, two Nimbusite Ingots and three Mistglow Dust.
