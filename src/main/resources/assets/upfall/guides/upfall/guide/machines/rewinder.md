# Rewinder

<BlockImage id="rewinder" scale="4" float="right" />

The **Rewinder** slowly repairs a tool or piece of armor placed on it, with no experience and no anvil.

## Crafting

Smith an Anvil, a <ItemLink id="kairite_gear" /> and a [Rewinder Blueprint](index.md).

<RecipeFor id="rewinder" />

## Using it

1. Fuel it: right-click with <ItemLink id="kairite_dust" /> or a <ItemLink id="kairite_gear" />. An empty-handed click shows the fuel percentage.
2. Right-click with any item that has durability to set it on top. One item at a time.
3. Wait. The machine restores **1 durability every 2 seconds** and spends one fuel point each time. A chime plays when the item is whole.
4. Click with an empty hand to take the item back.

A full tank holds 1000 points. A dust is worth 99 repairs and a gear 891. Enchantments and other data on the item are kept.

A comparator next to it outputs the item's remaining health (15 when it is undamaged). Breaking the Rewinder gives back the item and the leftover fuel as Kairite Dust.
