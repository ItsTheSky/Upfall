# Gadgets

Small tools that make travelling the sky safer.

## Updraft Compass

<ItemImage id="updraft_compass" scale="3" />

The <ItemLink id="updraft_compass" /> always points to the nearest Updraft Vent within 160 blocks, so you can ride the mist columns up to the next island. It only works inside the Upfall.

<RecipeFor id="updraft_compass" />

It can also be found in the Inverted Observatory and the Pearl Aerie, or bought from a Loftkin trader for 2 Nimbusite Ingots and 2 Mistglow Crystals.

## Nimbus Flask

<ItemImage id="nimbus_flask" scale="3" />

Use the <ItemLink id="nimbus_flask" /> to condense a short-lived platform of cloud under your feet (a disc about 5 blocks wide). It also cancels your fall distance, so it is a lifesaver when you slip off an island. It has a short cooldown and leaves you an empty glass bottle.

<RecipeFor id="nimbus_flask" />

## Cloud Pearl

<ItemImage id="cloud_pearl" scale="3" />

<ItemLink id="cloud_pearl" /> is dug from Pearled Cloud (see [Ores](ores.md)). It is used in the Nimbus Flask and the Cloud Lamp.
