# Gadgets

Small tools that make travelling the sky safer.

## Updraft Compass

<ItemImage id="updraft_compass" scale="3" />

The <ItemLink id="updraft_compass" /> always points to the nearest Updraft Vent within 160 blocks, so you can ride the mist columns up to the next island. It only works inside the Upfall.

<RecipeFor id="updraft_compass" />

It can also be found in the Inverted Observatory and the Pearl Aerie, or bought from a Loftkin trader for 2 Nimbusite Ingots and 2 Mistglow Crystals.

## Nimbus Flask

<ItemImage id="nimbus_flask" scale="3" />

Use the <ItemLink id="nimbus_flask" /> to condense a short-lived platform of cloud under your feet (a disc about 5 blocks wide). It also cancels your fall distance, so it is a lifesaver when you slip off an island. It has a short cooldown and leaves you an empty glass bottle.

<RecipeFor id="nimbus_flask" />

## Cloud Pearl

<ItemImage id="cloud_pearl" scale="3" />

<ItemLink id="cloud_pearl" /> is dug from Pearled Cloud (see [Ores](ores.md)). It is used in the Nimbus Flask and the Cloud Lamp.

## Fold Anchor

<ItemImage id="fold_anchor" scale="3" />

The <ItemLink id="fold_anchor" /> is dropped only by the [Fold Warden](../creatures/fold_warden.md), the guardian of the [Tilted Reliquary](../structures/tilted_reliquary.md) core. Use it to set an anchor, then use it again within 30 seconds in the same dimension to snap back to that spot with your fall distance reset. Each snap costs 1 durability (64 in all) and has a 5-second cooldown; repair it with a <ItemLink id="cloud_pearl" />.

## Gliders

<ItemImage id="down_glider" scale="3" />

Hold a <ItemLink id="down_glider" /> in either hand while falling to glide down slowly instead of dropping. Hold a direction key to fly 1.5 times as fast as sprinting, steering with your view. While you hold it, you raise both arms and carry the glider above your head. You take no fall damage while a glider is in your hand. It does not work while flying with Elytra, swimming or under inverted gravity. Each flight wears the glider by 1 durability per second spent gliding, up to 10 per flight, taken when you land. Repair it at an anvil with a Down Tuft; it accepts Unbreaking and Mending.

<RecipeFor id="down_glider" />

<ItemImage id="dusk_glider" scale="3" />

The <ItemLink id="dusk_glider" /> is an upgraded Down Glider that flies twice as fast as sprinting. Repair it at an anvil with a Duskwing Plume.

<RecipeFor id="dusk_glider" />
