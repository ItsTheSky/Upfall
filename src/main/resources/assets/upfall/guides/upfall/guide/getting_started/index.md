# Welcome to the Upfall

![The Upfall](../assets/vista.png)

**The Upfall** is a sky dimension stuck in the last golden hour of the day. Archipelagos drift above a sea of condensed clouds, whole islands hang upside down with their forests pointing at the abyss, and waterfalls pour *upward* into the rock. Gravity changes from one place to the next, and learning where it bends is how you get around.

It is built as an **endgame** destination. You need a Nether Star (so a Wither kill), an Elytra (so the End) and a Netherite Ingot just to open the door, and you arrive expecting to mine with netherite. In return you leave with two new gear tiers, new enchantments, machines and a few stories about the time you fell up.
