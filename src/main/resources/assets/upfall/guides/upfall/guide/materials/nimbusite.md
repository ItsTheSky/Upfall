# Nimbusite

Nimbusite is the cloud metal of the Upfall: lighter than netherite, yet better in every number. It is the first step of the equipment ladder and the base for [Levitite](levitite.md).

Mine [Nimbusite ore](ores.md) with a netherite pickaxe to get <ItemLink id="raw_nimbusite" />, then smelt it (blast furnaces work too).

## Smithing template

Nimbusite gear is upgraded from netherite gear. You need a <ItemLink id="nimbusite_upgrade_smithing_template" />, which is not craftable from scratch: find one in the loot of a Pearl Aerie (the best odds), the Inverted Observatory or the Drifting Ruins, or buy one from any [Loftkin](../creatures/loftkin.md): every Loftkin offers it once, for 10 Nimbusite Ingots and 4 Gildfruit. See [Structures](../structures/index.md).

Once you own one, you can duplicate it in a crafting table with 7 diamonds and a block of pearlstone (the template goes in the top middle slot); you get two.

## Equipment

At a smithing table, combine the template, a netherite tool or armor piece and a Nimbusite Ingot.

<RecipeFor id="nimbusite_pickaxe" />

The same recipe applies to the <ItemLink id="nimbusite_sword" />, <ItemLink id="nimbusite_axe" />, <ItemLink id="nimbusite_shovel" />, <ItemLink id="nimbusite_hoe" />, <ItemLink id="nimbusite_helmet" />, <ItemLink id="nimbusite_chestplate" />, <ItemLink id="nimbusite_leggings" /> and <ItemLink id="nimbusite_boots" />, each from its netherite counterpart. Nimbusite gear is fire resistant like netherite.

### Tools compared with netherite

| | Netherite | Nimbusite |
|---|---|---|
| Durability | 2031 | 2400 |
| Mining speed | 9 | 10 |
| Sword damage | 8 | 9 |
| Axe damage | 10 | 11 |
| Enchantability | 15 | 16 |

A Nimbusite pickaxe also mines [Levitite ore](ores.md), which netherite cannot.

### Armor compared with netherite

| Piece | Netherite | Nimbusite |
|---|---|---|
| Helmet | 3 | 3 |
| Chestplate | 8 | 8 |
| Leggings | 6 | 7 |
| Boots | 3 | 3 |
| Toughness | 3 | 3.5 |
| Knockback resistance | 10% | 12.5% |
| Total durability | 2035 | 2420 |

Nimbusite armor also has higher durability per piece and better enchantability.

## Set bonus

Wearing a full set of Nimbusite armor (helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots) reduces fall damage to **a quarter** of normal, which is welcome when the sea of clouds is far below.

## Next step

Upgrade your Nimbusite gear into [Levitite](levitite.md) for the best equipment in the Upfall.
