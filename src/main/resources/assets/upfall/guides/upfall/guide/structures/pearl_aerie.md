# Pearl Aerie

<ItemImage id="gravity_sceptre" scale="4" float="right" />

The Pearl Aerie is an **abandoned city of the sky builders**, floating high above the Drifting Archipelago: a tall keep
and a ring of smaller pearlstone towers joined by bridges, half of them crumbling. It is the only known source of the
**Gravity Sceptre**.

<br clear="all" />

## Finding it

- Only in the **Drifting Archipelago**.
- Very rare: roughly one per region of 96 by 96 chunks.
- It floats in open air between the island layers, around **Y 124 to 160**; you will need a way to cross the sky
  (see [The Upfall](../world/index.md)).
- The whole city can spread over about 100 blocks.

## Layout

<BlockImage id="weathered_pearlstone_bricks" scale="3" float="left" />

- **The keep:** the biggest tower, 15 to 17 blocks wide and 6 to 7 floors high, with a terrace on the roof. A ladder climbs
  through its floors, and an **Updraft Vent** at the base sends a column of Updraft Mist up through every floor, so you can
  ride up instead of climbing.
- **Towers:** four to eight in all. Medium towers (3 to 4 floors, some with a terrace) and small ones (2 to 3 floors, topped by a cone roof) cluster around the keep at different heights.
- **Bridges:** pearlstone walkways three blocks wide with low walls, lanterns and hanging glowvines. Long ones may widen
  into a **landing** with either a basin of skywater or a small obelisk.
- **Floors:** some floors are **libraries** (shelves and a lectern) and some are **hanging gardens**.
- Everything is lit by Cloud Lanterns, Mistglow Lanterns and a few Spent Lanterns, covered in cobwebs, moss and vines.

<br clear="all" />

## Dangers

- **Broken bridges.** About one bridge in three with a long span (9 or more blocks) has a section of 3 to 6 blocks missing.
  Bring blocks, or glide.
- **Weathered Pearlstone Bricks.** Some bridges, and patches of the keep floors, use <ItemLink id="weathered_pearlstone_bricks" />.
  About a third of a second after you step on one it **vanishes for good**, and it
  sets off every weathered brick next to it, one after the other. On a bridge the trap section is three blocks long: sprint across before it gives way
  or glide over it.
- **Spawners.** The keep has a Cragmaw spawner on the ground floor and a Duskwing spawner on the floor just below the treasure.
  Medium towers sometimes hold a Duskwing spawner on their top floor. Break the spawner with a pickaxe to stop
  the waves.
- **Arrow trap.** The keep treasure is guarded by up to three pressure plates in front of the chest. Each of them is
  linked to a dispenser full of arrows that shoots straight up. Break the plates before stepping on them (breaking a
  plate does not set it off), or shield yourself.

## Loot

Chests appear in the towers; the keep always holds its treasure on the top floor.

### Keep treasure (top floor of the keep)

- **Always:** the <ItemLink id="gravity_sceptre" />.
- **One of:** Nimbusite Upgrade Smithing Template (50%), Levitite Upgrade Smithing Template (25%) or Updraft Compass (25%).
- **4 to 7 rolls** among Nimbusite Ingot, Levitite Shard, Levitite Ingot, Cloud Pearl, Diamond, Gold Ingot, Ender Pearl and
  Enchanted Book.

### Armories (keep floors, some medium towers)

The keep holds two, on its second floor.

- One of: Nimbusite Upgrade Smithing Template (36%), Levitite Upgrade Smithing Template (7%), Updraft Compass (14%) or nothing.
- 1 to 2 of: Enchanted Book, enchanted Bow or Nimbusite Ingot.
- 3 to 5 rolls of Arrows, Spectral Arrows, Iron Ingot, Gold Ingot, Raw Nimbusite or Diamond.

### Tower chests

Small towers (60%) and medium towers hold tower chests, and the keep has several:
3 to 6 rolls among Raw Nimbusite, Nimbusite Ingot, Levitite Shard, Cloud Pearl, Lumen Shard, Mistglow Crystal, Gildfruit,
Cloudberry Tart, Gold Nuggets, Arrows and Feathers.

## The Gravity Sceptre

<ItemImage id="gravity_sceptre" scale="3" float="left" />

The Gravity Sceptre is an epic item with **250 uses**. Hold it and use it:

- **Use:** you become **Weightless** for **30 seconds**. Under a ceiling, gravity flips for you:
  you fall **up** and can walk on the underside of the ceiling or the island above you, as if it were the floor.
  Using it costs one durability.
- **Use again** while Weightless: you let go and the effect ends (no durability used).
- **Sneaking** also lets you let go of a ceiling.
- A 2-second cooldown applies after every use.
- Repair it on an anvil with a <ItemLink id="levitite_ingot" />.

<br clear="all" />

**Tips:**

- Any ceiling counts: the hanging underside of islands, the roof of a cave or the ceiling of a tower.
- Look out for the moment the effect ends: you fall back down. Do not switch off over a drop.
- The effect is the same Weightless that you find under inverted islands; read about it in the
  [world chapter](../world/index.md).
