# Skyray

<GameScene zoom="1" interactive={true}>
  <Entity id="upfall:skyray" />
</GameScene>

The Skyray is a wide, flat, harmless ray that glides lazily through the open sky. It is purely ambient: it never attacks, and it has no drops.

## Where it spawns

- Only in the Upfall, in open air between Y 72 and 240.
- Found in every biome except Gilded Ridge; the Mistsea has the most.
- Spawns in groups of 1 to 3.

## Behaviour

- Picks a spot up to roughly 28 blocks away in clear air and glides there slowly, bobbing gently, then picks another.
- Never goes below Y 72 or above Y 240.
- Ignores players entirely.

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Health | 12 (6 hearts) |
| Size | 1.6 blocks wide, very thin |
| Experience | 2 |

## Tips

- It shares its altitude band with the dangerous [Duskwing](duskwing.md), so a calm Skyray does not mean the sky is safe.
- Use a spawn egg to populate the sky around a base for decoration.
