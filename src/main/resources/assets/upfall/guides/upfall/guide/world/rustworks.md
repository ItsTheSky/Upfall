# Rustworks

An abandoned industrial region: pipes, valves and cogs the size of buildings, under a rusty fog with flakes of rust drifting in the air.

## Terrain

Rustworks form large, self-contained zones, separated from the rest of the archipelago by a gap of open sky. The islands are small and scattered, covered in **Oxide Turf** over Rustsoil and Rustslate. Huge walkable pipes bridge them.

## What you find

- **Ores**: <ItemLink id="kairite_ore" /> (found only here), with <ItemLink id="nimbusite_ore" /> and <ItemLink id="pearled_cloud" />.
- **Flora**: Rivetgrass, Verdigris Moss, glowing Verdigris Blooms, hanging Wirevines and Cloud Ferns.
- **Ironbark trees** with gear-like canopies.
- **Structures**: Pipe Bridges, giant valves, half-buried cogs, pipes plunging into the cloud sea, and the Stalled Engine. See [Structures](../structures/index.md).
- **Stalled automatons**: wrecked machines, some hiding a Sky Cache.

## Hazards and helpers

### Exhaust Vent

<Row gap="10">
  <BlockImage id="exhaust_vent" scale="3" />
  <ItemImage id="exhaust_vent" scale="2" />
</Row>

<ItemLink id="exhaust_vent" /> blocks puff steam in bursts and launch whatever stands above them upward, toward the island overhead. Each vent has its own rhythm: a burst of two seconds, then a pause of at least four. It glows while it bursts, and launches up to 18 blocks high if the way above is clear. Sneaking halves the launch speed, and you take no fall damage from the flight.

### Leaking Pipe

<Row gap="10">
  <BlockImage id="leaking_pipe" scale="3" />
  <ItemImage id="leaking_pipe" scale="2" />
</Row>

<ItemLink id="leaking_pipe" /> is a Rusty Pipe that drips a teal liquid. If an empty Cauldron sits directly below it (within 10 blocks, with nothing solid in between), the pipe slowly fills it into a Stillflow Cauldron.

### Valves, cogs and pipes

Giant valves, half-buried cogs and pipes plunging into the cloud sea are mostly landmarks. The pipe bridges are walkable. The metal blocks of the region are crafted from Rusty Plates; see [Blocks](../blocks/index.md).

## Creatures

Skyrays, Mistjellies, Cragmaws and Duskwings. No Skygrazers.
