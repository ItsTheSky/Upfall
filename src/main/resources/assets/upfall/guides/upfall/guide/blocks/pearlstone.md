# Pearlstone

Pale, calcite-like stone: the main body of every floating island and the default rock of the whole dimension. It is the most common building material you will find.

## Where to find it

- Pearlstone forms the core of nearly all islands and the needle spires under them; grass and soil only cover the top layer.
- Cobbled Pearlstone also turns up as rubble in the gas caves and in ruined structures.
- Polished Pearlstone and Pearlstone Bricks are what many Upfall builders used, from the Chronospire to abandoned sky towers.
- It needs a pickaxe. Mining Pearlstone drops **Cobbled Pearlstone**; use Silk Touch to keep the block itself.

## Crafting

- **Pearlstone**: smelt Cobbled Pearlstone in a furnace.
- **Polished Pearlstone**: 2x2 of Pearlstone, gives 4.
- **Pearlstone Bricks**: 2x2 of Polished Pearlstone, gives 4.
- **Cracked Pearlstone Bricks**: smelt Pearlstone Bricks.
- **Mossy Pearlstone Bricks**: combine Pearlstone Bricks with a Moss Block or a Vine (shapeless).
- **Chiseled Pearlstone Bricks**: two Pearlstone Brick Slabs stacked vertically, or Pearlstone Bricks in a Stonecutter.

## Stairs, slabs and walls

Cobbled, plain, Polished, Brick and Mossy Brick Pearlstone each have stairs, slabs and walls. Cracked and Chiseled Bricks do not.

Crafting gives 4 stairs, 6 slabs or 6 walls. A **Stonecutter** is cheaper: one block gives 1 stair, 1 wall or 2 slabs, and it can also cut Pearlstone or Polished Pearlstone straight into bricks and brick variants.
