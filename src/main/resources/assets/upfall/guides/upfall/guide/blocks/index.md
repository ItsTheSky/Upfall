# Blocks & Building

The Upfall offers a full palette of natural and building blocks: three stone families, three wood sets, soft cloud-colored canopies, and a garden of strange plants and lights. Most come with stairs, slabs and walls, and the stone families can be cut quickly in a Stonecutter.

## Where to start

- **Stone** is everywhere: the islands are made of [Pearlstone](pearlstone.md), the underside of the ridges and the deeper rock is [Duskslate](duskslate.md), and the rusted machine-land of the Rustworks is [Rustslate](rustslate.md).
- **Wood** comes from three tree species, one per kind of terrain. See [Wood & Canopies](woods.md).
- **Grass, soil and flora** are covered in [Soils & Plants](soils_and_plants.md); most plants need Skygrass, Skysoil or a cloud to survive.
- **Lighting** is in [Lights](lights.md).

> Nearly every block here needs a pickaxe if it is stone or metal, an axe if it is wood, and shears or bare hands for plants. The Cloud Lamp and Mistglow Crystal are the brightest blocks you can place.
