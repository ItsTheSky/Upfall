# Kairite

Kairite is the clockwork mineral of the Rustworks. It is the fuel of the blueprints and machines of the Upfall, and it rewards patience.

## Mining it right

<ItemLink id="kairite_ore" /> only generates in Rustslate in the Rustworks (see [Ores](ores.md)); an iron pickaxe is enough. The vein pulses slowly: about every 10 seconds it brightens, flares for roughly 2 seconds at its brightest, then dims again. Neighbouring ores share the same rhythm.

| Glow when mined | Drop |
|---|---|
| Dim or fading | 1 to 2 Kairite Dust (Fortune adds more) |
| Brightest | 1 Kairite Gear |

Silk Touch keeps the ore block. Time your swing on the flare to get gears directly.
