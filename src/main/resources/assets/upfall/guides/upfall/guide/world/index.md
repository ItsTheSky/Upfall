# The Upfall

![The Upfall at dusk](../assets/vista.png)

The Upfall is a dimension made of sky. There is no ground: only floating islands, a sea of clouds, and an abyss below that swallows anything that falls.

## A frozen golden hour

Time does not pass here. The sun hangs at dusk forever, the fog is warm and the sky glows. Monsters still spawn, but beds work at any time, so you can sleep whenever you like to set your spawn point.

## Layers

From top to bottom:

- **Upper islands**: a high layer of floating islands.
- **Lower islands**: a second layer of islands beneath the first. Some islands are **inverted**: their grass faces down, and you can walk on their underside (see [Gravity](gravity.md)).
- **The cloud sea**: a thick band of clouds with open holes onto the void. See [The Cloud Sea](cloud_sea.md).
- **The abyss**: nothing but void, pierced by rare glowing stone needles that rise from the depths.

Islands also hide **gas caves** carved inside their rock (see [Gas Caves](gas_caves.md)).

## Getting around

Without a way to fly you rely on the sky's own machinery: [Updraft Vents and Upfall Cascades](updrafts_and_cascades.md) carry you upward, [Dense Cloud](cloud_sea.md) breaks your falls, and the [void rescue](void_and_falling.md) saves you if you fall through anyway.
