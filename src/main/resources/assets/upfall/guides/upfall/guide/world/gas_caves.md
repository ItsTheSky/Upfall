# Gas Caves

Networks of rooms and corridors run deep inside the islands, filled with an invisible gas.

## Veil Gas

**Veil Gas** is a gas you cannot see without protection. It has no collision, so you walk through it, but when it reaches your **head** it hurts. By default it deals one heart of damage about once per second. The death message reads "breathed the Veil".

### Seeing and avoiding it

Wear a <ItemLink id="veil_mask" /> (or a Levitite helmet with the Veilsight upgrade). It filters the gas, so you take no damage, and reveals it as drifting motes. Without protection you only catch the occasional mote up close, so scout carefully and hold your breath on the way out.

## The caves

A gas cave is a set of rooms joined by corridors, carved deep in island rock. A thick shell of rock hides it from the outside: the only ways in are its one or two entrances, and the rock around each entrance glows with <ItemLink id="mistglow_crystal" />.

- **Cliff mouths** open on the flank of an island and slope down into the network.
- **Chimneys** rise into thick islands from their underside.
- **Spiral shafts** wind down from the island surface.

Rooms come in many shapes: low domes, tall cathedrals, rooms of several lobes, often with rock pillars and dripstone stalactites and stalagmites. Their walls are streaked with bands of Moonshell, Calcite and Tuff, with darker intrusions and worn, cobbled floors. Corridors are round tubes or narrow, tall fissures, with patches of gravel and hanging roots near the island surface. Small ore veins crop out of the room walls: coal, iron and Nimbusite, and sometimes diamond. Mobs never spawn inside the gas.

Some rooms are special:

- **Abandoned camps**: supply barrels, spent lanterns, an abandoned pickaxe, sometimes a cracked mask and bones. Supply barrels hold <ItemLink id="flicker_shard" />, <ItemLink id="kairite_dust" /> and <ItemLink id="bottled_veil_gas" />, and sometimes (10 percent) a Stalled Engine Map, a good lead toward the Kairite and the Veil Mask Blueprint.
- **Lumen grottos**: walls of Lumen Rock and Lumen Druse, a floor of Lumen Moss and sprouts, Glowvine hanging from the ceiling.
- **Quantum rooms**: walls bristling with Flicker Shard Clusters, and a pit hidden under a Crumbling Floor, with more clusters at the bottom.
- **Kairite seams**, in the Rustworks only: Kairite Ore in the walls and Verdigris Moss on the floor.

## Bottled Veil Gas

<ItemImage id="bottled_veil_gas" scale="2" />

Right-click gas with a Glass Bottle to collect it as <ItemLink id="bottled_veil_gas" />. Use the bottle on a block to release the gas on that spot; you get the empty bottle back.

## Flicker Shard Clusters

Crystal clusters grow on cave walls. Like everything Quantum, they only stay still while you look at them, and hop elsewhere when unobserved. Mine one while keeping your eyes on it to get a <ItemLink id="flicker_shard" />. See [Machines & Quantum](../machines/index.md).
